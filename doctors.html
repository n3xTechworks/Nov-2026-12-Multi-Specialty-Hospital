OPD appointments

How booking a consultation works

Consultation fees are shown on each profile and paid at the desk, by UPI or in the patient portal.

Step 1

Choose your doctor

Use the filters above, then press Book appointment to open the patient portal with that doctor selected.

Step 2

Pick a slot

Choose an OPD date and time, in person or by video. You get an SMS and email with your token number.

Step 3

Arrive 15 minutes early

Bring old reports and your medicines. Your prescription and test results appear in the portal afterwards.
