Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about appointments, visits and payments. There is more in our help centre.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

No. You can book any Northcrest specialist directly. If you have a referral letter or reports from another doctor, please bring them to your first visit.

How soon will my appointment be confirmed?

Our help desk calls within four working hours of your request, usually much sooner. Requests made after 20:00 are confirmed the next morning.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring a photo ID, your insurance or TPA card, previous reports and scans, and a list of the medicines you take. Arrive 15 minutes early to register.

Do you accept health insurance and cashless treatment?

Yes. We are empanelled with most major insurers and TPAs, as well as CGHS and corporate schemes. The insurance desk near Gate 1 handles pre-authorisation for planned admissions.

Can I have a video consultation instead?

Yes, for follow-ups and second opinions. Choose video when you book from your patient dashboard, or mention it in the reason for visit and we will arrange it.

What are the visiting hours for inpatients?

General wards welcome visitors from 11:00 to 13:00 and 17:00 to 20:00, two visitors at a time. ICU visits are limited to one relative for 15 minutes at set times.
