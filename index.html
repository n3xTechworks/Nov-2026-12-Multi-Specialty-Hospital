Questions

Questions patients ask us

Quick answers about our specialities, emergency care and the patient portal. There is more in our help centre.

Which specialities does Northcrest treat?

We have 12 departments, including cardiology, neurology, orthopaedics, oncology, paediatrics and gynaecology. See the full list on our departments page.

Is the emergency department open on Sundays and holidays?

Yes. Emergency and trauma care, the cath lab, imaging and the pharmacy are open 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Can I get a second opinion on reports from another hospital?

Yes. Book a consultation with the right specialist and bring your scans and reports, or upload them in the patient portal before your visit.

How do I see my lab reports online?

Log in to the patient portal and open Reports. Most blood test results appear the same day, and you can download or share them as a PDF.

Do you treat patients from outside Bengaluru?

Yes. Our patient coordinators help outstation families plan consultations, admission dates and nearby stays, and arrange airport pick-up on request.

Is there parking at the hospital?

Visitor parking is at Gate 1. The emergency entrance at Gate 3 has drop-off bays kept clear for ambulances and cars bringing patients in.
