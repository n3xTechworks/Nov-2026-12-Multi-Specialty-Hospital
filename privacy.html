Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal and health information.

Last updated 1 October 2026

Your health information is personal. This policy explains, in plain language, what Northcrest Multispeciality Hospital does with it. It applies to the hospital, this website and the patient dashboard. This is a sample policy for a demonstration website.

1. Who we are

Northcrest Multispeciality Hospital, 21 Outer Ring Road, Hebbal, Bengaluru 560024, is responsible for your information. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the medical record rules that apply to hospitals in India.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email and address, and those of your emergency contact and family members you add.
  • Health information: symptoms, diagnoses, consultation notes, lab and radiology reports, prescriptions, admissions and discharge summaries.
  • Billing details: insurance and TPA numbers, bills, claims and payment references. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Website and dashboard use: pages visited, device type and sign-in times, used to keep the service secure.

3. How we use it

We use your information to provide safe care, book and remind you of appointments, share reports and prescriptions with you, process payments and insurance claims, and send updates you have agreed to. We use anonymised data to audit and improve the quality of care. We never sell your information.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed, with: doctors and hospitals involved in your care, your insurer or TPA when you ask us to claim, laboratories that process your samples, and trusted IT providers bound by confidentiality contracts. We may share information when the law requires it, for public health reporting, or to protect someone's life.

5. How long we keep it

Outpatient records are kept for at least three years and inpatient records for at least seven years after your last visit, or longer when the law requires. Website enquiries that do not lead to a visit are deleted after 12 months.

6. Your rights

  • See and get a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Withdraw consent for reminders and health updates at any time.
  • Nominate a family member to act for you if you are unable to.
  • Raise a concern with our Grievance Officer, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

Records are stored on encrypted systems with role-based access, and every view of your record is logged. Staff receive privacy training each year. The patient dashboard uses secure connections and automatic sign-out.

8. Cookies

This website uses only essential storage to remember your theme, reading direction and sign-in session. We do not use advertising cookies.

9. Contact us

Write to our Grievance Officer at privacy@northcrest.example or call +91 80 4718 2200. You can also read our Terms of Service.
