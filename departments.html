Not sure where to start?

Three ways to reach the right specialist

You do not need a referral letter. If your symptoms span several specialities, our coordinators arrange one combined visit.

Option 1

Start with a physician

A general physician OPD runs every day. They examine you and refer you to the right department the same morning.

Option 2

Call the helpline

Describe your symptoms to a nurse on +91 80 4718 2200 between 7:00 and 22:00 and book a suitable doctor.

Option 3

Book online

Pick a department and doctor in the patient portal, choose a slot and get an SMS with your token number.
