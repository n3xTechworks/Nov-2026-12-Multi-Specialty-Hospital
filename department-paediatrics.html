About Paediatrics & Neonatology at Northcrest

Our children's wing has its own entrance, play areas and paediatric emergency room. Paediatricians, neonatologists and child specialists care for everything from routine vaccines to premature babies in our level III neonatal intensive care unit.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Ask anything else at your consultation or call the helpline.

Do you follow the national vaccination schedule?

Yes. We follow the IAP schedule and also offer optional vaccines. Bring your child's vaccination card to every visit.

Can parents stay with their child?

One parent can stay with a child at all times on the ward and in the NICU we encourage kangaroo care.

When should I bring my child to the emergency room?

Come straight away for breathing difficulty, a fit, a very drowsy child, a high fever in a baby under 3 months or signs of dehydration.

Do you have weekend clinics?

Yes. The paediatric OPD is open every day including Sundays and public holidays.
