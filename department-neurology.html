Questions

Frequently asked questions

Ask anything else at your consultation or call the helpline.

What are the warning signs of a stroke?

Remember BE FAST: Balance loss, Eyes (vision loss), Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call 108 or our emergency line.

Do I need an MRI before my first visit?

No. The neurologist will examine you first and order only the tests you need.

Is spine surgery always necessary for a slipped disc?

Most people improve with medicines and physiotherapy. Surgery is advised only when there is nerve weakness or pain that does not settle.

Can I drive with epilepsy?

Rules depend on how well seizures are controlled. Your neurologist will advise you and provide a fitness letter when it is safe.
