Appointments

How do I book an appointment?

Use the request form on our Contact page, call the helpline on +91 80 4718 2200, or log in to your patient dashboard to pick a doctor, date and slot instantly.

Can I reschedule or cancel an appointment?

Yes. Open Appointments in your dashboard and choose Reschedule or Cancel, or call the helpline. Please give at least four hours' notice so another patient can use the slot.

Do you offer video consultations?

Yes, for follow-ups, report reviews and second opinions. Choose Video when booking in the dashboard. You will get a secure link by SMS and email 15 minutes before the call.

How long will I wait on the day?

Most patients are seen within 20 minutes of their slot. Your token number and live queue position appear on the screens outside each OPD and in your dashboard.

Medical reports

When will my lab reports be ready?

Routine blood tests are ready the same day, usually within six hours. Cultures take two to three days and biopsy reports five to seven working days. We send an SMS when each report is ready.

How do I view or download my reports?

Log in to your dashboard and open Reports. You can view values with normal ranges, download a PDF or share a secure link with another doctor.

Can I get my scan images (CT, MRI) on a disc?

Yes. Request a copy at the radiology desk or from your dashboard. Digital images are also available through a secure viewer link at no extra cost.

How do I get my discharge summary or medical records?

Your discharge summary is handed over on the day you leave and added to your dashboard. For a full copy of your records, apply at the medical records desk with photo ID; copies are ready within three working days.

Billing and insurance

Which insurers and TPAs do you accept?

We are empanelled with most major health insurers and TPAs, plus CGHS, ECHS and many corporate schemes. Ask the insurance desk near Gate 1 to confirm your policy before a planned admission.

How does cashless admission work?

Bring your policy card and photo ID. Our insurance desk sends the pre-authorisation request to your insurer, usually approved within four to six hours. You pay only non-covered items and any co-pay at discharge.

How can I pay my bills?

Pay by UPI, card or net banking in your dashboard under Billing, or at any billing counter. You receive an itemised receipt by email straight away.

Can I get an estimate before a procedure?

Yes. Ask your doctor's coordinator or the billing desk for a written estimate. It lists the room category, procedure, implants, medicines and expected length of stay.

Visiting hours

What are the visiting hours for wards?

General and private wards welcome visitors from 11:00 to 13:00 and 17:00 to 20:00. Two visitors are allowed at a time, and one attendant may stay overnight.

Can I visit a patient in the ICU?

One close relative may visit for 15 minutes at 12:00 and 18:00. The ICU doctor briefs the family every day at 16:00 in the family lounge on Level 3.

Are children allowed to visit?

Children under 12 may visit general wards with an adult, but not the ICU, NICU or oncology day-care, to protect patients from infection.

Is there food, parking and a place to stay for families?

The ground-floor cafeteria is open 7:00 to 23:00, and paid parking runs 24 hours. Our help desk can suggest nearby guest houses with patient discounts.
