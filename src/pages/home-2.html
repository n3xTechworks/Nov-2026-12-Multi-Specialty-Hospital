Your patient journey

Three steps from booking to recovery

Whether you come for a check-up or surgery, a named coordinator keeps every step on time and explained.

  1. Step 1{{icon clock 16}}About 10 minutes

    Book and check in

    Book online or call the helpline. On the day, scan your QR code at the kiosk and your token appears on the screen.

Prepare for your visit

What to bring on the day

A short checklist for each kind of visit, so nothing holds you up at the desk.

{{badge stethoscope}}

OPD consultation

For a first visit or follow-up with any specialist.

  • Photo ID and appointment token
  • Previous reports and scans
  • List of medicines you take
  • Arrive 15 minutes early
{{badge flask}}

Health check-up

For any preventive package booked online or at the desk.

  • Fast 10 to 12 hours, water is fine
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing
  • Bring your glasses or hearing aid
  • Carry morning medicines to take after
{{badge bed}}

Planned admission

For surgery or a hospital stay your doctor has booked.

  • Insurance card and admission note
  • Pre-authorisation letter, if any
  • Clothes and toiletries for your stay
  • One attendant with a companion pass
